What you can do

A real RPG with a world that keeps its author's 1988 voice: his maps, his characters, his villain.

🛡️

A company that grows

Retainers rise from hired hand to shield of the realm. They carry their own light, loot the dead and take the best gear after you, in order of loyalty.

🗺️

68 chapters from the binder

Twenty holds traced room by room from the original graph paper, the rest built from each hold's own binder facts.

🔥

Tear it down

With eight retainers at your back, or the arms of a hill giant, pull down thrones, watchtowers and the enemy's siege engines.

🐎

Survive the road

Food and water for every mouth, horses, ponies and a wagon, camps with a watch, and weather you can see.

🎮

Play your way

Keyboard and mouse, touch, or a gamepad that works every menu. Four camera views, from over the shoulder to first person.

📖

Books and films

A 225-page illustrated guidebook beside the game, short films for the great moments, and an illustrated book for every chapter.

Will it run for me?

Almost certainly. If your browser can show a 3D map, it can play Duchame.

  • Any modern browser with WebGL: Chrome, Edge or Firefox on Windows, macOS or Linux.
  • Desktop or touch. On a tablet or phone, a joystick appears on screen.
  • No install and no account. Press Play and you are in.
  • Saves stay in your browser, in ten slots. Clear your browser's site data and they go with it.
  • Free. Support the game on Patreon if you enjoy it.

🎮 Playing with a controller

Any Xbox-style pad works and drives every menu. For the most from it, an 8BitDo Pro 2 in its Windows (XInput) mode. A D-pad-only pad plays the whole game through the quick menu.

Left stick or D-padWalk, and move through menus
Right stickTurn the camera
A / BTalk / Jump
X / YSearch / Inventory
Start / SelectQuick menu / Map

Switch it on or off from the title screen, or add ?pad=on / ?pad=off to the game's address.
